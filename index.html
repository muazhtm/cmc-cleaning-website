About us

A cleaning partner that pays attention to the details

Our team has been in the cleaning business since 2009. In 2019 we brought that experience into CMC Cleaning Services (M) Sdn Bhd. We provide high-quality cleaning and customised maintenance programmes suited to the needs of each organisation, and we hold our staff to high standards of cleaning and customer service.

We work closely with our clients so their needs are anticipated and understood. Because we stay at a size where clients get personal attention from senior management, issues are resolved quickly and standards stay consistent.

  • Customised cleaning programmes at cost-effective rates
  • Equipment regularly checked and tested for electrical safety
  • Environmentally friendly materials, used by trained staff
  • Supervised teams with low staff turnover for consistent quality

Specialist work

High-rise cleaning, done safely

High-rise window and façade cleaning is dangerous work without the right training. Our technicians are specially trained in rope access and skylift methods, and renew their safety training every year.

  • Every technician is attached to two independent safety lines anchored to the roof
  • All tools are tethered to the technician, so nothing falls to the ground below
  • Work planned to protect people and property around the building

How we work

Systems behind every clean

Our on-site results are backed by training, supervision and clear procedures that we keep improving.

Health & safety

Safe systems of work in line with the Occupational Safety and Health Act 1994, with policies reviewed regularly.

Colour coding

Cloths, mops and buckets are colour coded by area to prevent cross-contamination between toilets, kitchens and offices.

Training

Staff are trained before and on site, then for each site's specific needs, including safety and security procedures.

Eco-friendly

Microfibre cloths and mops reduce chemical use, and we follow recycling and environmental policies on site.
